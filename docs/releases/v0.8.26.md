# Ariadne v0.8.26

Backup failures stay visible until a successful backup completes. New failed
attempts produce notifications even when the warning text has not changed.

## Fixed

- Include failed or unverified backups in CLI and tray health independently of
  newer maintenance progress. Waiting for a retry remains a warning.
- Keep failed maintenance stages visible until a later successful maintenance
  outcome resolves them. Safely deferred completion remains distinct from failure.
- Record one final backup outcome for both `backup` and `backup-full`.
- Identify the recovery command that failed instead of reporting an unnamed exit.

## Notifications

- Show the current issue directly in the tray menu.
- Notify on distinct failed attempts, including failures during scheduled work.
- Retry when the operating-system notification command fails, and record its
  result in the tray log. Native notification permissions and Focus settings apply.
- Include the command error and log guidance when manual maintenance fails.

## Non-destructive repair

`ARIADNE_BACKUP_PRESERVE=1` explicitly preserves the previous verified full,
independent recovery copies and snapshot verification workspaces, and skips
legacy/native pruning for that process and its children. New full sets still
require independent recovery checks. Normal one-full retention is unchanged;
preserved repair evidence requires a separately approved cleanup plan.

Audit, manifests and recovery logs remain available outside rotation. No existing
extra artifacts are silently removed by installing this release.
