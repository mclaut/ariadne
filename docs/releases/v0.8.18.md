# Ariadne v0.8.18

The tray access menu now separates requests that need a decision from retained
history. Requests no longer get buried below a long flat list of old records.

## Changed

- The access section appears near the top of the tray menu.
- Only pending requests appear under **Needs your decision**. Each request keeps
  its own exact-ID Approve and Deny controls.
- **Access history** shows all other records newest first, ten per page, with
  record ranges and dates. New records appear on the first page.
- Rows show a short purpose and status. Full project scope, resource, creation
  time, and ID are available in bounded detail lines.
- History rows are read-only; sorting or reusing a display slot cannot retarget
  an authorization action. Ambiguous global approval buttons are gone.
- All seven supported languages include the new menu labels and shorter states.

## Preservation and compatibility

All request, decision, consumption, and audit files remain intact. Completed rows
leave the active queue and remain accessible in history. Grant deadlines, scope
and session binding, explicit MCP retries, and one-time enforcement are unchanged.

## Verification

- Regression coverage with 155 old records and new pending requests.
- Decision-to-history transition, complete pagination, immutable input, and exact
  resource/ID preservation, including consecutive spaces in paths.
- Full Go tests, targeted race checks, build, lint, and rendered EN/UK website checks.
